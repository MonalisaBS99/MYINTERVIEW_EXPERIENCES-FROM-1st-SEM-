# 🎯 My Interview Preparation Journey

> Documenting my journey from 1st semester to placements — 
> learning, preparing, making mistakes, and growing every day.

Welcome to my interview preparation repository! 🚀

This repository is my personal record of my engineering journey, 
from the first semester to internship opportunities and final placements.

Instead of keeping my experiences, preparation strategies, and 
interview learnings to myself, I want to document everything in one 
place so that other students can learn from my journey too.

## 👩‍💻 About Me

- 🎓 B.E. Computer Science and Engineering Student
- 💻 Learning Data Structures and Algorithms using C++
- 🧠 Exploring core computer science subjects
- 🚀 Preparing for internships and software engineering placements
- 📚 Focused on consistent learning and practical understanding

## 🎯 Purpose of This Repository

The goal is to document my complete journey, including:

- Semester-wise academic experiences
- Internship and placement preparation
- Interview experiences and questions
- Data Structures and Algorithms preparation
- Technical skills and projects
- Resources that helped me learn
- Mistakes, challenges, and lessons learned
- Preparation strategies that worked for me

This repository will grow as I progress through my engineering journey.
