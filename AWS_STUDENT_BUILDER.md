# ☁️ AWS Student Builder Group – Interview Experience

## 📌 Introduction

This repository documents my interview experience for the AWS Student Builder Group.

I am sharing my experience to help other students prepare for similar opportunities and understand the types of questions they might encounter during the interview.

## 🎯 Interview Experience

The interview was a valuable learning experience that helped me reflect on my understanding of cloud computing, my interests, and my motivation to join the community.

### 📝 Questions Asked

### 1. Tell me about yourself.

I introduced myself as a Computer Science Engineering student and shared a little about my academic background and interests.

**Tip:** Prepare a short introduction covering your background, technical interests, and learning goals.

### 2. What do you understand by the AWS Student Builder Group?

The AWS Student Builder Group is a student community that encourages students to explore cloud technologies, learn about AWS, develop technical skills, and connect with other learners.

It provides an opportunity to grow, explore new technologies, and become more involved in the cloud computing ecosystem.

### 3. Why do you want to join the AWS Student Builder Group?

I wanted to join because I am interested in learning new technologies, exploring cloud computing, and improving my technical knowledge.

I see this opportunity as a way to learn, connect with a technical community, and grow through practical exploration.

### 4. What do you understand by cloud computing?

Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services such as servers, storage, databases, networking, and software over the internet.

Instead of purchasing and maintaining all the physical infrastructure ourselves, we can access computing resources whenever we need them.

**Examples of cloud platforms:**
- Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- Microsoft Azure
- Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

**Real-life example:**

Google Drive allows us to store files online and access them from different devices without needing to store every file locally on each device.

Cloud computing offers flexibility, scalability, and convenient access to computing resources.

### 5. Which other cloud platforms do you know apart from AWS?

I mentioned Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure.

These are two major cloud computing platforms apart from AWS.

## 🎉 Interview Result

**Status: SELECTED! ✅**

I was happy to get selected for the AWS Student Builder Group.

This opportunity motivates me to explore cloud computing further and strengthen my understanding of AWS services.

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Be prepared to introduce yourself clearly.
- Understand the community or program you are applying for.
- Be ready to explain why you want to join.
- Understand the fundamentals of cloud computing.
- Be confident while communicating your thoughts.
- Stay curious and be willing to learn.

## 🚀 What's Next?

I plan to continue exploring AWS services and strengthening my cloud computing fundamentals.

My focus is on learning concepts clearly, gaining practical knowledge, and making the most of this opportunity.
